Why Instagram won Stories

product teardown

Snapchat invented the format; Instagram won it. It took all three of: making posting easier, a reason to check back every single day, and a huge audience Instagram already had to put Stories in front of. Remove any one and the product dies. A borrowed audience cannot save a product people would not return to on their own, and a new product has no borrowed audience to begin with.

Big platforms can carry weak features. New products cannot.

product teardown

Instagram can afford features that merely keep people a little more engaged, because Instagram already supplies the crowd. A new product shipping the same feature on its own dies, because that one feature has to be the entire reason anyone shows up. Before copying a giant's feature, ask what is actually holding that feature up.
